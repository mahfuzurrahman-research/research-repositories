## Research Repositories

### 🔒 citizen-evidence-revisable-record

**Citizen Evidence and the Revisable Administrative Record: Unequal Visibility, Verification and the Limits of Correction**

A formal and computational study of how citizen-generated evidence enters, persists within, and challenges administrative records under unequal visibility and verification conditions.

**Repository status:** Private — source code, computational materials, provenance records, and research files are not publicly accessible.

---

### 🔒 who-should-navigate-the-state

**Who Should Navigate the State? Administrative Burden, Navigation Responsibility, and the Organizational Allocation of State Complexity**

An empirical public-administration study of how governments allocate navigation responsibility and administrative complexity across citizens, organizations, and institutional processes.

**Repository status:** Private — source code, analytical materials, provenance records, and research files are not publicly accessible.

---

### 🔒 where-the-clock-starts

**Where the Clock Starts: Administrative Timeliness in a Multistage Public Program**

An empirical study of administrative timeliness that examines how the definition and location of the institutional starting clock shape measured delay across a multistage public program.

**Repository status:** Private — source code, analytical materials, provenance records, and research files are not publicly accessible.

---

### 🔒 complaint-paradox

**The Complaint Paradox: Administrative Observability and the Limits of Complaint-Based Performance Measurement**

A computational and empirical study of the conditions under which citizen complaint volumes may diverge from the underlying state of public-service performance.

**Repository status:** Private — source code, analytical materials, provenance records, and research files are not publicly accessible.

---

## Access

Detailed code, data, model specifications, computational workflows, provenance materials, and manuscript-linked outputs remain access-controlled.
